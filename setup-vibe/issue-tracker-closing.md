## Closing implementation issues

Before closing, complete the **Processes** and **Disk** steps in `## Working habits` (stop every process you started, empty the temporary folders).
When an implementation ticket is finished, set its status line to `done` (keeping the file's format, e.g. `**Status:** done`) and commit that change on its own.
A ticket whose status is `done`, `resolved`, or `wontfix` is closed and out of triage scope.
Wayfinder decision tickets live under `.scratch/plan-*/` and are never implementation tickets.
